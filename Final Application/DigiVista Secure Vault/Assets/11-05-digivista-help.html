DigiVista Secure Vault Help

Simple guidance for individuals, families and business teams.

Privacy promise: DigiVista administrators must not know or store your master password. Vault contents remain encrypted. Keep your recovery material offline and secure.

Start safely

  1. Create your own memorable master password; do not reuse it anywhere else.
  2. Enable two-step login and store its recovery code offline.
  3. Prepare the recovery kit, add a private password hint and register an approved recovery option.
  4. Confirm that encrypted backups run to a separate device or customer-controlled location.
  5. Create one test record and confirm that you can view, edit and delete it.

Choose the correct category

Personal Accounts

Private personal credentials. Do not place these in a company collection.

Desktop & Laptop

Device name, local/domain username, owner and review date.

Host/IP, environment, privileged account, owner and expiry date.

Router, switch, Wi-Fi and VPN credentials with asset/location details.

Security Appliances

Firewall, endpoint, monitoring and other security-console accounts.

Banking & Finance

Highly restricted personal or approved finance-team records.

Shared Team Credentials

Business credentials shared only through authorized organization collections.

Home and family use

Use private folders for Websites, Personal, Banking, Wi-Fi, Devices, Documents and Secure Notes. Share only selected records with a family collection; private records remain private.

Business access model

Owner / Super Administrator

Manages the organization, policies, recovery enrollment and access reviews. This role does not reveal users' private vault contents.

Administrator

Invites users and assigns approved groups/collections. Users create their own master passwords.

Department User

Uses only assigned collections and their own private vault.

Read-only / Limited User

Receives only the minimum view or edit rights required for work.

If you forget the master password

Email can deliver a hint or an approved recovery workflow, but it cannot reveal the old master password. Try a previously unlocked trusted device, configured passkey, emergency access or pre-enrolled organization recovery. Without a configured recovery method, the encrypted personal vault cannot be decrypted.

Backup and restore

Back up the full persistent data directory, attachments, sends, cryptographic keys and deployment configuration. Keep an encrypted copy away from the Vault server and test restoration regularly. A server backup key and a user's master password are separate secrets.

Employee change or exit

Revoke sessions, remove group/collection access, rotate shared credentials and preserve required audit evidence. Never ask the departing user to disclose their master password.

Need support?

Contact the designated DigiVista/customer administrator for configuration help. Never send a master password, recovery key, TOTP secret or vault export through email or a support ticket.
