# DigiVista Secure Vault — Legal and Policy Approval Pack

Date: 11-06-2026  
Status: **Prepared for qualified legal/privacy review; not a legal opinion or certification**

## Product statements approved for review

- Vaultwarden remains the secure vault engine; DigiVista does not claim ownership of Vaultwarden.
- DigiVista branding and guidance do not remove Vaultwarden/AGPL attribution.
- DigiVista does not know a user's master password and must not claim it can recover an unconfigured zero-knowledge vault.
- Personal vault items and Private Journal entries are private by default; organization roles do not create a decryption backdoor.
- The product is **designed for alignment** with data-minimisation, purpose-limitation, access-control, security, retention, and user-rights principles.
- The product must not claim “ISO/IEC 27001 certified,” “GDPR compliant,” or “DPDP compliant” without the required organisational evidence and qualified approval.
