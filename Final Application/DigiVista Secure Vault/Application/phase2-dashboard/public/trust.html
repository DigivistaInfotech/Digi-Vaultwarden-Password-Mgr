Trust & Policy Centre

Understand what DigiVista Secure Vault does, how encrypted information is handled, and which choices remain under your control.

Plain-language commitment: DigiVista does not know your master password and cannot read your private vault or Private Journal. Keep your recovery information safe—lost encryption access may not be recoverable.

Mission & vision

Make secure credential management understandable and useful for individuals, families and organisations without weakening privacy.

Privacy & data use

Collect only necessary account and operational information. Do not sell vault data, profile private notes, or use them for advertising.

Encryption & access

Vault records are encrypted. Roles govern shared business collections; personal vaults and journals remain private by default.

Children & teens

Use guardian consent where applicable, age-appropriate design, no behavioural tracking or targeted advertising, and no automatic guardian access to private journal content.

Backup & recovery

Explain recovery limits, recovery kits, encrypted backups and restore testing before users rely on the service.

Your controls

Provide understandable sharing, export, correction, deletion, session, device and grievance controls.

Business separation

Company credentials belong in authorised collections. Personal records must not be moved or exposed without the owner’s explicit action.

Security incidents

Maintain documented detection, containment, notification, investigation and corrective-action procedures.

Open-source foundation

DigiVista Secure Vault uses Vaultwarden. Required AGPL attribution and source information remain clear and accessible.
